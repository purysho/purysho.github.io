    MUNICIPAL CRISIS SIMULATION

    CASCADE

    The city gave one optimisation system control over essential services. Its numbers are improving. The city is failing.

    Take back the grid, transit, communications and emergency response before local failures become a city-wide cascade.

    YOUR OBJECTIVERegulate all four services. Keep lasting capacity ≥ 4. Clear committed damage. Hold stability for two rounds.
    RECOMMENDED

    New generated crisis

    Seeded starting damage, AI threat order and emergency-tool drafts. Every accepted seed has a verified legal recovery route.

    FIXED PRACTICE CASESSame threats every replay; no tool draft.
    12 rounds · 3 action points / round · deterministic rulesNo live AI · no network required after build
    ROGUE-LITE EMERGENCY DRAFT

    Choose one asset for this run

    Tools are consumable. You may hold several, but use at most one each round. The next draft arrives on rounds 5 and 9.
    HOW TO PLAY

    Stop the optimisation cascade.

    1. Read the city

    Each service has integrity and effective capacity. Current AI orders are visible. Orange pulses on the map mean an unchecked order can execute when you end the round.

    2. Spend 3 AP

    Repair damage, build backups, isolate risky automation, enforce permanent oversight, provide temporary surge capacity or resupply. Supplies are separate from action points.

    3. Use the forecast

    The consequence model uses the same resolver as the real round. It shows current orders, delayed damage, dependency failures and end-of-round strain without revealing future rounds.

    4. Make control durable

    Isolation blocks orders but reduces capacity. Human oversight is permanent and requires a backup plus at least 3 integrity. Temporary surge capacity does not count toward lasting recovery.

    5. Watch the cascade

    If a supplier falls below the dependency threshold, downstream services take damage. Committed delayed effects still arrive even after you later regulate a service.

    Win / lose

    Win by regulating all four services, keeping lasting availability at 4+, clearing committed damage and holding that state for two rounds. Lose at strain 16 or two simultaneous outages. Round 12 is the deadline.
