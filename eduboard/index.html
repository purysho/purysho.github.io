BUILT AROUND THE TEACHING DAY

One place for the work that usually gets scattered.

EduBoard is deliberately practical: the information a teacher needs every week should not live across disconnected spreadsheets, cloud logins and one-off documents.

01

Students & classes

Keep one student directory, class rosters, contact information and course-specific grading rules.

02

Gradebook & rubrics

Enter scores in a spreadsheet-style gradebook, reuse rubrics and keep a change history for recorded marks.

03

Attendance

Mark Present, Late, Absent or Excused and see attendance rates without maintaining another tracker.

04

Lesson planning

Store objectives, materials, activities and homework beside the classes and assessments they belong to.

05

Reports

See class averages, pass rates, grade distributions and attendance trends, then create printable student reports.

06

Import, export & backups

Bring in rosters from Excel or CSV, export gradebooks, and create restorable database backups.

LOCAL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

The app and the data belong to the teacher.

EduBoard uses a local SQLite database and does not require a hosted backend. In portable Windows mode, the database can live beside the executable, making the application and its data movable together between machines.
